Key Recurring Messages From the Session

Evidence, evidence, evidence. The Home Office "can't read minds" — years of residence, work, tax records, children's schooling, medical treatment, and any ongoing personal risk all need to be submitted in writing, ideally with a covering letter.

The pause on Syrian decisions has been lifted, but the backlog it created means decisions are still taking longer than normal. Every case is decided on its own individual merits, not as a block.

While an application is pending, your prior lawful status continues and no one will be arrested, detained or removed. Leaving the UK, or asking for documents back to travel, is normally treated as withdrawing the application.

Never switch routes by withdrawing a current application without independent legal advice — it can leave you in the UK unlawfully in the meantime.

Two items are being followed up by the Home Office team outside the call: the 18-month case with no Notice of Intention, and general accountability contacts for the Syrian backlog. Updates were promised via the organisers.
